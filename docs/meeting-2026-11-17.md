# 1117 Meeting

## Decisions

- The app runs on the server, we connect to it using SSH
- Ditto is the avatar backend we invest in now. Wav2lip and MuseTalk stays as a fallback (only lipsync, no emotion control)
- TensorRT engines built with TRT 10.13 on the box, not the prebuilt TRT-8.6 "Ampere Plus" lane
- Serving resolution: 960p (720p and 960p measured equal; 1080p not viable)

## Technologies

- Ditto (LMDM motion + warp + decoder), registered per avatar from a source video
- TensorRT 10 fp16 engines for all three lanes: LMDM (ONNX export), warp as a plugin-free hybrid split around its two GridSample nodes, decoder
- HuBERT features from streaming PCM (PcmASR) as the audio→motion condition
- WebRTC via aiortc (H.264/libx264), Flask/aiohttp server, WSL2 + RTX 3060 box

## Achieved

- Ditto backend fully wired: registration → artifact → streaming, with tests
- Throughput, measured end to end on the box: 4 fps → 13.6 fps (512²) from the three TRT lanes; render_size=256 lane closes the gap → 25 fps delivered = real-time speech (but abandoned)
- Serving at 960p, ~22–24 fps, ~1% dropped frames
- Emotion conditioning: per-utterance via `/human` `emotion` field (cannot leak past sentence end) plus session-sticky `/set_emotion`; real eye state captured at registration
